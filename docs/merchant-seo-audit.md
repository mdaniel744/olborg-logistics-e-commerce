## Priorities requiring verified facts or operational work

### P0 — Complete before treating the store as ready for Merchant Center review

1. **Resolve seller address and legal particulars.** Obtain the current official KRS extract, verify registered address, registry court and representation, and identify a valid correspondence address. Confirm VAT status separately where needed; a NIP is not evidence of an active VIES registration. Apply the same legal identity to footer, contact information, terms, returns, privacy, checkout, order communications and invoices. If the contact address differs from the registered office, label the distinction. The new policies intentionally do not invent a registered office. Review the information duties for this Polish seller and its German-directed offer. [Google misrepresentation policy](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127?hl=en), [Polish Consumer Rights Act](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2024/1796/text.html), [German provider-information rules](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html).

2. **Set the actual return depot and specialist transport process.** Confirm the address that can physically accept a container, its contact person, booking/arrival process, unloading arrangements and whether customers may choose their own suitably equipped carrier. Make this information available before the purchase and in the return instructions. A correspondence address must not be presented as a depot. Logistics coordination must not make statutory withdrawal conditional on seller approval. Current email instructions are usable, but are not a substitute for the missing operational destination. [Google return-policy requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12756116?hl=en), [EU consumer rights directive, Articles 6 and 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj).

3. **Provide an advance return-cost amount or defensible maximum estimate.** For a container that cannot normally be returned by post, a generic “calculated individually after return request” is insufficient. Verify costs by delivery country, location, container size and required lifting equipment, including relevant taxes. Show the amount/maximum estimate before a binding consumer order and retain the information with that order. The owner's consumer-pays policy depends on proper prior disclosure; it is not a basis for retroactive charges. Where the required disclosure was absent, statutory cost protections remain. No return-price range has been invented in the content. [EU consumer rights directive, Article 6(1)(i) and model instructions](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj), [UOKiK on return costs](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-odstapienia-od-umowy/koszty/).

4. **Confirm delivery times and charges by market.** Supply order processing/handling time, transit time for Poland and Germany, applicable business days, dispatch cut-off/timezone if used, and the process for non-stock containers. Differentiate availability from an agreed delivery deadline. Confirm automatic zone prices cover actual routes and whether crane unloading is included. The 30-day consumer delivery fallback in the revised policy is a statutory default when no other period is agreed, not an operational handling/transit promise. Merchant Center shipping configuration must match the website and checkout. Unknown delivery must not be treated as zero in a payment-binding total. [Google requirements for full cost and checkout](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12756116?hl=en), [EU consumer rights directive, Articles 6 and 18](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj).

5. **Verify bank details, invoices and the external order system.** Confirm PLN/EUR beneficiary names and account details match the legal seller, establish which document accepts an order, and verify the delivery of bank instructions and VAT invoices. The storefront submits orders to an external dashboard; this local audit does not verify that dashboard's invoice templates, outgoing mail or actual refund process. Preserve server-authoritative prices and totals. Do not advertise card/PayPal payments unless they are really enabled. [Google checkout/payment requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12756116?hl=en).

6. **Review the electronic withdrawal requirement for German consumers.** Current German BGB §356a provides for a visible online withdrawal function, a separate confirmation action and a prompt durable acknowledgment with the declaration content, date and time. The local shop currently describes email withdrawal and provides a textual form; a mail link is not the same as that function. Determine the rules applicable to this Polish business's German-directed contracts, then implement and test the required flow with reliable submission storage and acknowledgment delivery. Do not display a form that pretends a notice was accepted when no durable delivery exists. [Current German BGB, §356a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/BJNR001950896.html), [Article 246a EGBGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgbeg/art_246a__1.html), [Directive (EU) 2023/2673](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/2673/oj).

   Polish implementation needs a separate current check: PARP's 11 August 2026 guidance reported that the UC82 project had been withdrawn and explained that the directive does not itself impose a direct horizontal obligation on private traders before national implementation. Do not infer a Polish infringement solely from the EU's 19 June 2026 application date. Recheck enacted Polish law before launch. [PARP: one-click withdrawal, implementation status](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/91021:one-click-return-czyli-zwroty-w-e-commerce-po-nowemu).

7. **Inspect the live catalogue and Merchant Center account.** Validate that every advertised variant can actually be purchased, that stock and selected condition are genuine, and that price, VAT, currency, language, landing URL and image agree between feed, product page and checkout. Quote-only and demo records should not be submitted as available retail offers. Compare account shipping/return settings with the final verified policies, then inspect actual item/account diagnostics. The local code review is not evidence that those account settings have changed. [Google Merchant Center guidelines](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12756116?hl=en), [Google misrepresentation policy](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127?hl=en).

### P1 — Privacy and operational safeguards

8. **Protect uploaded customer photos.** At audit time, `src/app/api/uploads/route.js` writes quote attachments under `public/uploads/` and returns directly accessible URLs. A random filename is not access control. Review already-uploaded files, storage permissions, retention/deletion and provider access. Decide on private storage with authorized retrieval or another properly secured workflow. Do not promise attachment confidentiality while retaining a public delivery path. No private photos were uploaded during this review.

9. **Complete the data-processing inventory.** Identify the live hosting, order/inquiry database, email, invoice, backup and analytics providers; controller/processor roles; actual processing regions; any non-EEA transfers and safeguards; and enforceable retention/deletion periods. Source confirms use of Supabase for inquiry/database services, but not hosting region or transfer safeguards. These facts are not fabricated in the privacy policy. Generic recipient categories alone cannot resolve an unverified transfer situation. [European Commission on transparency obligations](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/obligations_en).

10. **Verify deployed browser storage and consent behavior.** Local source uses `olborg_cart_v1`, `olborg_lang` and `olborg_cookie_consent` in localStorage, plus `olborg_quote_draft_v3` and `olborg_last_order` in sessionStorage. It does not include Google Analytics or advertising pixels. The revised popup informs users about necessary storage rather than collecting fictitious analytics/marketing choices. Reopening it shows the same active-storage information; browser-storage errors no longer prevent dismissal. Check deployed tags/CDN injections separately; if optional tracking is later added, document it and gate it before any consent-dependent loading.

11. **Substantiate product claims.** Verify actual per-unit condition, dimensions, weight and payload, floor material, certifications/CSC status, stock and unit photographs. Avoid making “waterproof,” “cargo worthy,” “certified,” guaranteed lead-time or adaptation claims at category level unless substantiated. A normal size or RAL selection does not automatically make stock goods legally customized. Ensure product descriptions edited in the external catalogue are also accurate; local fallback text does not override all remote records.

## Policy/content changes made locally

- Replaced the old incorrect seller styling and the claim that missing registration data would appear when an administrator supplied it. Policies now use the owner-confirmed full legal seller and numbers, separately from the brand.
- Reworked delivery text around actual container planning: country/address, vehicle access, crane reach, unloading responsibility, stable support, total charges and a pre-agreed deadline. Removed vague lists of unrelated uses from the cross-border delivery section.
- Clarified the 14-day withdrawal period and separate 14-day return period, the email declaration process and nonmandatory model form, pre-contract oversized-return cost information, refund by the same payment method, ordinary outbound delivery reimbursement, and the earliest goods/proof-of-return withholding rule with the pickup exception.
- Removed language that could make a driver's damage report or immediate notification a condition of consumer rights. Explained separate statutory remedies and seller-paid necessary corrective transport, including for used goods.
- Removed the blanket implication that all wear described in a used listing can never be a defect. Specified that any deviation must meet the applicable disclosure/agreement requirements.
- Distinguished consumer/qualifying sole-trader protection from ordinary B2B returns. Avoided treating a standard size/condition/color choice as an automatic custom-goods exception.
- Made privacy copy more specific to the forms, data recipients and browser storage visible in the code. Added accessible links between delivery, returns, withdrawal, contact and privacy/cookies.

Files for these changes: `src/i18n/policies.js`, `src/features/storefront/PolicyPage.jsx`, `src/features/storefront/DeliveryPage.jsx`.

## Intentional local safeguards / release blockers

`SITE_SETTINGS.returns.transport_estimates.PL` and `.DE` are deliberately `null` because the business has not supplied a defensible estimate. Consumer checkout displays the reason and a quotation link instead of accepting an uninformed order. The feed returns HTTP 503 until the selected market has its estimate and a verified HTTPS `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is configured. **Do not connect this paused feed to a live Merchant Center fetch and assume it is ready.** All deployments lacking that domain configuration, local development, and explicitly enabled demo deployments are non-indexable. No production configuration was modified.

After obtaining confirmed amounts/maxima, enter translated, VAT-inclusive text per market as described in the README, verify it across product/checkout/policy communications, complete every P0 item and test the live feed with actual product data before enabling scheduled fetching. Private customers must ultimately be able to complete checkout; a quotation-only storefront is not made Merchant-ready by an XML feed.

### Additional operational mismatches to resolve before release

- The external checkout payload currently sends product IDs/quantities but no explicit local delivery cost or local VAT treatment; the dashboard's accepted total is not reconciled with the local displayed total. Verify the external API contract and implement a server-authoritative agreement of product price, delivery, VAT and invoice total before processing real payments. Do not simply trust a browser total or invent unsupported dashboard fields.
- The configured Polish/German VAT defaults and automatic VIES-based B2B treatment still need accountant review against actual registrations, destination-tax obligations and evidence of an intra-Community supply. Removing the inaccurate “reverse charge” label does not prove eligibility for 0% VAT.
- Production stock, translations, product photographs and configured providers could not be validated with the local demo catalogue. Existing data backups and uploads were not deleted or migrated.
